## התוכן הגולמי כפי שנאסף

> **Healthcare Paradox**: Despite "high life expectancy," the system faces "erosion" with workforce shortages and bed/equipment deficiencies.

> **Heat Stress & Health (2010-2023)**: Study documents effects on "emergency room visits, hospitalizations, and mortality" linked to heat exposure across Israel.

> **Economic Strain**: Macroeconomic report notes increased "government budget deficit, the level of debt relative to GDP" due to war costs.
